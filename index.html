Our Story

"Tifa's not just about dessert — it's about connection, sharing laughs, playing games, and creating moments."
— Naina Balupari, Owner & Founder
🤍 Locally Made 🦙 LaMa Llamas ☕ Groundwork Coffee

A Walnut Creek
Original

Originally from India, Naina Balupari and her family have proudly called California home for over 20 years. A software engineer turned gelato entrepreneur, Naina opened the first-ever Tifa Chocolate & Gelato in Walnut Creek with a dream: build something that brings people together.

The store's beloved llama mascot is a Balupari family tradition — a playful blend of daughters Laasya and Maanya's names ("LaMa"). Keep your eyes open — llama figurines and artwork are hidden all around the store!

Every batch of gelato is crafted in-house daily on Locust Street. You're not getting a franchise frozen delivery — you're tasting something made this morning, with love.
